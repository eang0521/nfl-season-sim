What is this?

A simulator built on ESPN's Football Power Index (FPI) offense/defense ratings and the real NFL schedule. Games are scored as 22 ± 1 (home/away) + offense − opponent defense, drawn from a normal distribution (stdev 8), then matched to the nearest of 10 randomly drawn historical NFL scores so results look like real final scores.
